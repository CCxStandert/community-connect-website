# The deal (our borrowing agreement)

*Community Connect × Standert · version 2 · November 2026*

Hey, nice to have you here. Before you roll off on one of our bikes, here's how this works. It's not legal small print, just how friends share something they love.

## Why we do this

Community Connect is a non-profit bike collective in Cape Town. Together with Standert we keep a small fleet of premium gravel and road bikes that **anyone can borrow**. You don't have to be fast, famous or part of the crew. We do our best to keep the bikes available as often as we can, so more people get to ride.

The whole idea is simple: **share the joy of riding a really good bike, and meet people who love it as much as you do.** If you end up riding with the person who handed you the bike, even better.

## Before you ride

- **Request the bike on our website.** You can ask for any dates up to the end of next year (right now: 31 December 2027). We check the calendar and confirm by email, usually within a couple of days.
- **We always keep one day free between loans.** That's our day to check the bike and pass it on calmly, so please don't ask us to squeeze bookings back to back.
- **Handoffs happen on WhatsApp.** We'll put you in a chat with the rider before or after you, or with the crew, to agree a time and place.

## We look after the bikes

We maintain every bike together with our friends at **Woodstock Cycleworks**. **Normal wear is on us**: chains, tyres and brake pads that wear out from riding, a puncture on a long day out, a cable that stretches. That's what bikes do, and that's what we're here for.

## Your part: bring it back like you got it

- **Same condition back.** The bike comes back the way you received it: clean, chain lubed, everything still on it.
- **Dirty or neglected?** If a bike comes back muddy, dry-chained or not looked after, you cover a **service or a wash & lube** at Woodstock Cycleworks. Fair's fair: the next person deserves a bike that's ready to go.
- **Broken because of reckless riding?** If something breaks because the bike was ridden carelessly, crashed while messing around, or used for something it isn't made for, **the repair is on you**. We'll have it fixed at Woodstock Cycleworks and share the invoice with you. No markup, no drama.
- **Something feels off? Tell us early.** A strange noise, a wobbly wheel, a scratch you're not sure about: message us. Small things caught early stay small.
- **Back on time.** Someone else is usually waiting for the bike. Need more time? Ask us before your end date and we'll see what we can do.

## Racing and events

Taking a bike to a race or an event? Brilliant, tell us which one when you ask. **Racing is harder on a bike than riding**, so there's one extra rule you tick for it: **any damage that happens while racing, crashes included, is yours to cover.** We get it fixed at Woodstock Cycleworks and share the invoice, no markup. Everything else in this agreement stays the same.

## Raffle bikes

Some events run raffles with us: the winner gets a bike for the race, and sometimes the entry, nutrition or kit too. **We hold those bikes back until the winner is drawn**, sometimes right before the event. You'll see them marked as raffle bikes in the calendar. If a raffle bike isn't claimed, it goes back into the pool and you can ask for it.

## Handing over between riders

When you collect a bike from the rider before you, **check it together**. Look at the tyres, brakes, gears, frame and wheels, and that everything's there. If anything isn't in decent shape, **tell us in the WhatsApp chat right then**, ideally with a photo. Whatever isn't mentioned at the handover, we assume you received in good condition.

## Share what the bike let you do

We love photos, short videos, a GPX file, a few lines about where you went, who you met or what you finally dared to ride. Tag **@cc_x_standert** or send it to us. It helps us show what this project makes possible.

You don't need to tell us how great the bike rides (unless you really feel like it). We care much more about the ride, the place and the people.

## Your details

We keep your name, email, WhatsApp number, height and loan history so we can arrange this loan and future ones. We don't sell or share them. Ask us anytime to see or delete them.

## In short

Ride it, enjoy it, look after it, bring it back clean, and tell us what you got up to. Then pass it on.

**Ride hard, look after it, pass it on.**

*Questions? Reply to any of our emails or message us on WhatsApp.*
