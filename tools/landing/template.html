    Events

    Gravel races with dates out. Pick one on the request form and we plan the bike around it.

      Racing terms

      Racing is harder on a bike than riding. For an event you tick one extra line: damage while racing, crashes included, is yours to cover. Repairs at Woodstock Cycleworks, invoice shared, no markup.

      Raffle bikes

      Win a bike, an entry, sometimes kit and nutrition. Some bikes stay held for organisers’ raffles until the winner is drawn. They show as raffle bikes and go back into the pool if nobody claims them.

      Dates as published by the organisers. Always confirm on the event’s own page before you enter.

      The deal, between friends

      We keep the bikes in shape. You bring them back the way you got them. Who pays?

      Who pays?

      We cover
      • Regular servicing with Woodstock Cycleworks
      • Normal wear: chain, tyres, brake pads
      • A puncture on a long day out
      • Finding you a bike as often as we can
      You cover
      • Breaks from reckless riding or the wrong kind of use
      • Damage while racing, crashes included
      • A wash & lube if it comes back dirty
      • Telling us early, and flagging damage at the handover

      Any bike will do?

      Ask for any bike in your size. We pick one that’s free on your dates.
