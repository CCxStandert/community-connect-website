Clothing and gear from our rides, races and friends (“merch”), and sponsorships: donations to one of our funds. Each product page describes the item, its sizes and its price. Colours can look slightly different on your screen. We make small runs, so stock is limited.

3. Prices and payment

Prices are in South African rand. The total you pay, including shipping, is shown before you pay. [We are not registered for VAT, so no VAT is added.] You pay through Yoco by card or Instant EFT. Yoco handles your card details on its own secure page; we never see or store them. Your order is confirmed when the payment goes through, and you get a confirmation email.

5. Changed your mind? 7 days to cancel

You may cancel a merch order, without giving a reason, within 7 days of receiving it (section 44 of ECTA). Email us with your order number, then send the item back unworn, unwashed and with its tags. You pay the direct cost of returning it. We refund the full amount you paid within 30 days of your cancellation, to the card or account you paid with.

6. Faulty or wrong items

If something arrives faulty, damaged or not as described, or becomes faulty within six months of delivery, send it back at our cost and you choose whether we repair it, replace it or refund you (section 56 of the CPA). Normal wear and damage from misuse are not faults.
