Community Connect shares bikes, organises rides and sells a little merch to keep it all going. To do that we need some personal information. This page says what we collect, why, who else sees it, how long we keep it and what your rights are. We keep it short and plain.

Who we are

Community Connect NPC (registration number 2025/713989/08), 2 Park Street, Graaff-Reinet, 6280, Eastern Cape, South Africa. Email contact@community-connect.cc, phone +27 76 223 0218. We are the “responsible party” for your information under POPIA.

Information Officer: Julian Robinet, contact@julian-robinet.de.

What we collect

  • When you borrow a bike: your name, email address, WhatsApp number, Instagram handle (if you give one), your height (to pick the right size) and, if you give it, your weight (for tyre pressure). We keep the dates and plan of each loan, the bike, the handoff, and notes about the bike’s condition when it comes back. If you join the waiting list, we keep your place in it.
  • When you order merch: your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and what you ordered.
  • When you sponsor: your name, email address, the fund, the amount and any note you add.
  • When you pay: Yoco processes your card or Instant EFT payment on its own secure page. We see that you paid and how much, never your card details.
  • When you write to us: your emails and WhatsApp messages.
  • When you visit the website: only if you press “Accept” in the cookie notice, Google Analytics measures which pages are visited, roughly from where (country and city), on what kind of device, and actions like adding to cart or requesting a bike. Our host keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

Why we use it

  • To lend you a bike: check dates, pick a size, arrange the handoff, remind you of the return date and look after the fleet. We need this to carry out the borrowing agreement you accept.
  • To send your order, or arrange collection at a community ride, and to deal with returns.
  • To confirm your sponsorship and report, in general terms, where the money went.
  • To answer your questions.
  • To keep financial records, because the law requires it.
  • With your consent only: to measure how many people the website reaches, so we can show supporters and sponsors what the project does. We report numbers, never names.

We don’t sell your information and we don’t send you marketing unless you’ve asked for it.

Who else sees it

Only what each one needs, and only to do the job for us:

  • Our crew (volunteers who run the fleet) and the rider before or after you, who get your first name and WhatsApp number to arrange a handoff.
  • Yoco, to process payments.
  • Our courier [name of courier], who gets your name, address and phone number to deliver an order.
  • Woodstock Cycleworks, our workshop, if a repair needs to be linked to a loan.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), when we message you there.
  • Host Europe, which hosts the website and its database in Germany, and our email provider [name], which delivers our emails.
  • Google: Google Analytics, only if you accepted it, and Google Fonts, which provides the website’s typefaces and so receives your IP address when a page loads.

Outside South Africa. Our website is hosted in Germany, which is covered by the EU’s data protection law (GDPR). Google and Meta may process information in the United States and elsewhere under their own data protection terms. POPIA allows this where the information stays protected to a similar standard.

How long we keep it

  • Your rider details: while you borrow bikes from us, and for [2] years after your last loan. You can ask us to delete them sooner. Then we remove your details and keep only the loan dates, so we know how much each bike was ridden.
  • Orders, payments and sponsorships: [5] years, because tax and company law require financial records.
  • Analytics data: [14] months in Google Analytics.

How we protect it

The website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Only crew members who need it can log in to see rider and order details, each with their own account. We never store card details.

Cookies

  • Essential, always on: they keep your cart and checkout working (woocommerce_cart_hash, woocommerce_items_in_cart, wp_woocommerce_session_…), let our crew log in (wordpress_…) and remember your cookie choice (cc_consent).
  • Analytics, only after you press “Accept”: Google Analytics (_ga, _ga_…), kept for up to 2 years. Press “Essential only” and Google Analytics doesn’t load at all.

Change your mind any time with Cookie preferences, also in the footer of every page.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us:

  • whether we hold information about you, and for a copy of it;
  • to correct it, or to delete it when we don’t need to keep it any more;
  • to stop using it, or to withdraw a consent you gave (such as for analytics).

Email our Information Officer at contact@julian-robinet.de or write to contact@community-connect.cc. We may ask you to confirm who you are, and we reply within 30 days.

If you’re unhappy with how we handled your information, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa):

  • Complaints POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za (with the POPIA complaint form from their website)
  • General enquiries enquiries@inforegulator.org.za · 010 023 5200 · toll-free 0800 017 160
  • Address Woodmead North Office Park, 54 Maxwell Drive, Woodmead, Johannesburg, 2191
  • Website inforegulator.org.za

Riders under 18

If you’re under 18, a parent or guardian needs to agree to the loan and to us keeping your details. They can use all the rights above on your behalf.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top. For bigger changes, we’ll let riders know by email.
